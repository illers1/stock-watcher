The same data as the watchlist, read the other way: which of these look weak on price, valuation, fundamentals, coverage and insider behaviour. Crowding is reported separately, because a company can deserve to fall and still be a ruinous short.

Shorting is not the mirror image of buying. A long position can lose what you put in; a short can lose several times it, because a price has no ceiling. You also pay to borrow the stock, owe any dividend paid while you are short, and can be closed out early if the lender recalls it. The screen below ranks weakness on public data — it does not say a stock will fall, and it cannot tell you what a position should cost you.
